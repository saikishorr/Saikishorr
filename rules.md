## Bowling & Batting Rules
- Run-Up: No run-up is allowed while bowling.
- If a player is a left-handed bowler, their left leg should be positioned forward while delivering the ball. Similarly, for a right-handed bowler, the right leg should be placed ahead during the bowling action.
- A bouncer above shoulder height is allowed once per match.
- A ball bowled above head height is a No-Ball.
- Runners: No runners are allowed.
 
## Female Bowling (1 Mandatory Over)
- All Deliveries: All deliveries bowled by female players will be counted.
- Wide Ball: In case of a Wide Ball, 2 runs will be awarded to the batting team.
 
## Boundary & Ball Rules
- Ball Outside Net: If the ball goes outside the net, 2 runs will be credited to the batting team.
- Overthrows: Runs from overthrows will be counted.
 
## Fielding & Catch Rules
- Behind Umpire: No more than 3 fielders are allowed to field behind the umpire.
- Fielder catches the ball and touches the front net behind the umpire = Counts as SIX RUNS
- Fielder catches the ball and touches the side net = Counts as Out
- Batsmen hit the Ball and the ball touches the top net after that fielder Catches the ball = Not Out
